# BookVoices Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 1, 2026  
**App:** BookVoices (`app.bookvoices`) — local audiobook player

## Summary

BookVoices does **not** collect, sell, or share your personal data with the developer or third parties for advertising or analytics.

## Stored on your device

- Your library, playback progress, bookmarks, tags, and listening history — in a local database on your phone.
- Settings (theme, skip intervals, language) — in local storage.
- Audio files stay where you put them; the app only remembers folder access permission.

## Network

By default the app works **fully offline**.

You may optionally enable **WebDAV sync** (Nextcloud, Synology, etc.). In that case:

- the app connects **only to the server URL you provide**;
- a JSON snapshot of progress is transferred (not audio files);
- credentials are stored on device; the password is encrypted via Android Keystore when available.

## In-App Purchases

The app may offer one optional in-app purchase: the BookVoices Pro Lifetime license. All payments and financial transactions are processed securely and directly by **Google Play Billing**. The developer does not receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.

## What we do not do

- No BookVoices user account required.
- No third-party advertisements or trackers.
- No analytics SDKs (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.).
- No collection of contacts, location, microphone, or camera data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.

## Changes

Policy updates are published at the URL listed in Google Play Console.
